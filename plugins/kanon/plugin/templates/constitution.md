# Constitution

The principles this project does not negotiate. Changing one of them is a
decision of its own, never the side effect of another change.

## Article 1 — Every acceptance criterion follows EARS and uses glossary terms

Each criterion is one sentence in one of the six EARS patterns, with the
keywords in uppercase English and the body in the language of this project. The
system it names and every domain term it marks exist in the glossary.
